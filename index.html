Structural & Thermal Analysis

Femur biomaterials study — Analyzed the mechanical behavior of femur biomaterials in ANSYS, comparing Co–Cr alloys and PMMA on load-carrying capacity and stress distribution to guide material selection.

Thermoelectric generator in a jet engine — Ran thermal-structural analysis and parametric optimization of a TEG under heat-transfer constraints, reaching ~6–7% conversion efficiency in the validated design window.

Blood plasma separation (microfluidics) — Simulation-driven design validation in ANSYS with hex-dominant meshing, achieving numerical convergence at 1e-11 and confirming feasibility of the micro flow-path.
